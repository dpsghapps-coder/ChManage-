# Presbyterian Church of Ghana (PCG): Presbyteries and Districts

The **Presbyterian Church of Ghana (PCG)** operates under a four-tier governance structure:
1. **General Assembly** (Supreme governing court)
2. **Presbyteries** (Regional / territorial administrative jurisdictions — **21 in total**)
3. **Districts** (Pastoral administrative clusters overseeing local churches — **over 380 church districts**)
4. **Congregations & Preaching Posts** (Local assemblies)
